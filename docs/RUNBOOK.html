Tool changer: real-arm runbook

SO-101 follower + ARM-9 v4 changer. Follow the steps top to bottom. Every go step moves the arm and asks you to type MOVE. Run everything from toolchanger\swap in the lerobot venv. Written 2026-10-24; nothing on this page has run on the arm yet.

E-STOP: Ctrl-C in the run window, the big STOP button (run_swap.py stopbutton), or run_swap.py stop in a second window.

The arm holds torque where it is (roll drops to 25 %, gripper stays capped). It never parks on its own after a stop. Then you choose: PARK (only if the tool is not in the dock), LIMP (support arm + tool first), or ENTER = exit holding.

The 12 V switch drops the arm. Use it only with a hand under the arm.

2
Twist ladder at the service pose
needs your go


The arm leaves rest, holds the coupling pointing forward about 200 mm up, roll at the entry angle. Only the roll moves. Each twist waits for your ENTER: present the tool at the entry angle, hold its body firmly, keep fingers off the plate and skirt. The reaction is at most 2.5 N·m, about a jar lid.


%PY% run_swap.py --dry-run --speed 1 twist      (rehearse, no port)
%PY% run_swap.py --speed 0.5 twist --levels 45 --tries 1
%PY% run_swap.py --speed 1 twist --levels 45,55,65,75,85 --tries 3


It prints the roll protection registers first. If it says PROTECTION BLOCK it stops before moving; read the line, then decide.


Watch: the body stays still while the roll turns; no smell, no buzzing past the twist; roll temperature in the printout below 55 °C.


Pass: locks 3/3 at 65 % or less, 10/10 unlocks at 85 % (--levels 85 --tries 10), rest error within ±2.5°. Note each peak load (N·m). If it needs 75 %, cut the overtravel to 2° before step 6.

%PY% run_swap.py --dry-run friction
%PY% run_swap.py friction

4
Teach the dock (arm limp, by hand)
needs your gohands


Tool locked on the master. Support the arm, type LIMP: the arm joints go limp, the blade keeps its capped torque. Seat the tool in the dock (neck on the land, body on the tail rest), hold the arm so it does not hang on the tool, ENTER. It records 3 times (re-seat between). Lift out and lay the arm on its rest. Torque stays off.


%PY% run_swap.py teach --dock post1


Pass: spread 0.3° or less per joint. Over 1° is refused as a mis-seat. The file carries the calibration fingerprint, so a re-calibration forces a re-teach.

First drop, half speed
needs your go


Tool locked on the master, arm at rest. One hand near the arm, the other on Ctrl-C or the STOP button.


%PY% run_swap.py --dry-run swap --drop post1     (twin rehearsal)
%PY% run_swap.py --speed 0.5 swap --drop post1


Watch: it goes up and over to a point 100 mm above and 60 mm back from the dock, comes down to 45 mm, slides over the slot, goes back once to weigh the tool (first drop of a session), returns over the slot, lowers in, presses about 1 mm, twists 49°, backs straight out 30 mm, then weighs again without the tool. Stop if the tool touches anything on the way in, if anything scrapes, or if it pushes hard into the dock.


Pass: 1/1 actions ok, tool sitting flat on the tail rest, exit 0 (parked, torque released at rest).

needs your go

Watch: the blade spins to its entry angle while far from the dock (normal), the master comes in on the axis, the blade searches (short ticks), then the roll twists. A wiggle of ±4° follows. Then it lifts 3 mm, waits, lifts out and weighs.

Cycles
needs your go


%PY% run_swap.py --speed 0.5 swap --cycle 5 --dock post1
%PY% run_swap.py --speed 1.0 swap --cycle 5 --dock post1      (only after 5 clean at 0.5)
%PY% run_swap.py --speed 1.0 swap --cycle 20 --dock post1


Twin result for this exact code: 11/12 seeds complete at 0.5, 10/11 at 1.0. The one repeat miss is the loaded lift-out after a pick (shoulder can't keep up): it ends in a hold with the tool on the master. If it happens on the arm, answer PARK once you have looked.


Milestone 1: 20 drop + pick cycles in a row, no hand help, no NEEDS_ATTENTION. Milestone 2: at least 48 of 50 over 2 sessions with a power cycle between, every failure ending in a hold.






What the code does when something goes wrong



SituationWhat the arm does


Any fault while the tool is engaged with the dock (inside the dock zone or a contact move)Holds in place: body keeps its gravity support, push into the obstruction is dropped, roll to 25 %, gripper re-capped, P gain unchanged. No park. Exit 4. Menu: LIMP or exit holding (PARK refused).


E-stop anywhereSame hold. Menu offers PARK only when not engaged.


Routine says the lock or the weight check failedHold + menu (the tool may be half on the master). PARK after you have looked.


Error outside contact (bus glitch, watchdog in free air)Backs out along the axis to the away pose, then sweep's normal park; torque released at rest.


Bus deadServos keep their last goal (torque on). Exit 4. Support the arm before reconnecting: lerobot's connect drops torque while it configures.




Exit codes: 0 ok (parked) · 1 error, parked · 4 NEEDS ATTENTION, torque held · 6 plan rejected before motion · 7 watchdog, parked.






Limits and watchdogs

    

  • Gripper Torque_Limit 85 (0.25 N·m) for the whole session, read back every 2 s; roll 25 % free, 55 % insert, 85 % only for the twist.
    • 

  • Command limiter: body 25°/s and 100°/s², roll 90°/s. When the routine asks for more, it waits until the command catches up, so paths and holds keep their shape.
    • 

  • Jump guard: a commanded step over 6° per tick (roll 10°) is treated as a bug and refused.
    • 

  • Tracking |ref − q| over 3° + that joint's measured friction band, for 3 ticks while engaged → hold. sweep's 12° following-error rule everywhere; outside contact a watchdog trip also holds (you choose PARK).
    • 

  • Body load over 70 % for 0.4 s while engaged → hold. Roll stall: 90 % of its limit and 10° behind → hold.
    • 

  • Temperature over 55 °C on 2 reads → stop.
    • 

  • Far transfer legs run at P 32 (sweep's park gain); everything near the dock runs at P 16, as in the sim, with a 1 s settle after the switch.
